Privacy Policy

Draft prepared 1 November 2026 · Nigeria

Draft — business-owner review required. Some operator details and operational policies must be confirmed before this document can serve as a final public notice or agreement.

This draft explains how the Ọka Rides booking and dispatch service in Awka, Anambra State, handles information belonging to customers and drivers. The responsible operator’s registered legal name and address still require confirmation.

1. Information we collect

  • Customer account and contact details: name, phone number, email address, login identifiers and account information.
  • Bookings: pickup and destination, selected time, passenger count, ride notes, ride references, driver assignment, status and ride history.
  • Location: pickup and destination map coordinates when provided, and operational location updates from drivers. Browser geolocation should be used only when permission is granted.
  • Driver records: identity and contact details, vehicle and plate information, availability, assignment history, earnings and verified payout bank details.
  • Payments: fare amounts, transaction references, verification and payout status. Customers entering payment card information on Paystack’s hosted checkout provide it to Paystack; the app’s hosted-checkout integration does not ask customers to enter full card details directly.
  • Technical information: session identifiers, service security information and data needed for request handling and fraud prevention.

2. Why we use this information

We process information to create accounts, arrange and track rides, contact customers and drivers, dispatch trips, verify fares and payments, reconcile driver earnings, respond to support requests and maintain security. Depending on the activity, the lawful basis may be performing a contract, complying with the law, a legitimate interest assessed against your rights, or consent where required.

3. Location and ride data

Pickup and destination information is available to the dispatch team and the driver assigned to the booking as needed to provide the ride. Driver location updates may be used for operational assignment and ride progress. The specific frequency, availability and retention of driver-location history require owner verification; this draft does not claim continuous background tracking.

4. Who receives information

Authorised dispatch staff and assigned drivers receive the details needed to complete a booking. Service providers supporting the system include Supabase (database/authentication), Vercel (hosting), Paystack (checkout and bank verification), Termii (SMS) and OpenStreetMap/Leaflet-based maps (map display). Google sign-in may be used when a customer chooses it. Each provider may process information according to its own applicable notice and agreement. Actual international data transfers and safeguards need to be verified.

5. Session cookies and device permissions

Customer and dispatcher sessions use cookies to support sign-in and account security. The current customer session implementation is designed around a 14-day session cookie; that does not mean booking records are deleted after 14 days. You may manage cookies through your browser, but blocking essential session cookies can stop account features. Browser notification and location permissions are optional and can be managed in device/browser settings.

6. Security and storage

The service uses server-side access controls and authenticated sessions to reduce unauthorised access. No online service can guarantee absolute security. A complete security and third-party processing review should be performed before final publication.

7. Data retention

We should keep records only for legitimate operational, accounting, dispute-resolution and legal purposes. The actual retention periods, deletion procedures and backup retention schedules have not yet been confirmed. These must be supplied before this policy is finalised.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable exceptions, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 provide rights including information, access, correction, objection, deletion, restriction and data portability, plus withdrawal of consent where consent applies. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Questions, access and deletion requests

Contact the dispatch/support team using the Support option in the Ọka Rides customer app and explain your request. The dedicated privacy email, controller legal name and physical contact address must be confirmed before publication. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing account information.

10. Changes to this notice

Material changes will be reflected in an updated notice with a new publication date; any additional notice required by law must also be provided.
