The ledger allocates 85% of each completed, paid fare to the driver and retains 15% for Oka Rides before Paystack processing fees. New Paystack and OPay rides both require manual bank payouts. Any old Paystack split transactions are excluded until independently reconciled. Confirm sufficient settled funds before transferring; record the transfer only after it succeeds.
