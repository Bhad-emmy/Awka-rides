Business name: Ọka Rides. These draft terms describe the operation of Ọka Rides, a Keke booking and dispatch service in Awka, Nigeria. They are not a final agreement until Ọka Rides has approved the missing commercial and legal details and confirmed its legal registration information.

1. Service and eligibility

Ọka Rides connects ride requests with a human dispatcher and available drivers. Users must provide accurate details and must be legally capable of making bookings or have a responsible adult make arrangements on their behalf. The minimum age and any additional driver qualification rules require confirmation.

2. Accounts and accurate information

Keep sign-in credentials private, use accurate contact details and promptly report suspected unauthorised access. We may need to verify a driver’s identity, vehicle and bank details before assigning rides.

3. Booking and driver assignment

Submitting a request does not guarantee immediate availability or confirm a particular driver or pickup time. A dispatcher reviews the request, assigns an eligible driver and confirms the fare. Customer and driver should use the confirmed trip details and notify dispatch if material details change.

4. Fares and payments

A fare is confirmed through the dispatcher-led flow before the customer is asked to complete payment. The app supports Paystack hosted checkout and, where offered, a dispatcher-verified offline payment method. Payment status is subject to verification; do not assume that a screenshot or an unverified transfer confirms payment. Actual pricing rules, surcharges and final supported methods need owner confirmation.

5. Cancellation and refunds

Contact dispatch or in-app support as soon as possible to request cancellation or report a payment problem. Cancellation fees, deadlines, refund eligibility, refund timing and no-show rules have not been approved and are not stated in this draft. They must be decided, disclosed and reviewed before these terms are finalised. Applicable statutory rights remain unaffected.

6. Customer responsibilities

Provide a safe, identifiable pickup point and correct destination, follow reasonable safety instructions, treat drivers respectfully, and do not arrange unlawful or unsafe trips or carry prohibited materials.

7. Driver responsibilities

Drivers must maintain required licences, roadworthiness and legal compliance, operate safely, respect customers, protect personal information, and keep vehicle and availability details accurate. Drivers should not share customers’ contact or location details for purposes unrelated to a trip.

8. Safety and service limitations

Road and traffic conditions can affect pickup and arrival times. Users should report safety incidents promptly to appropriate emergency services and to dispatch. No statement here promises that every ride will be incident-free or that any particular insurance policy applies; insurance arrangements must be confirmed by the operator.

9. Prohibited conduct and account action

Fraudulent payments, harassment, threats, misuse of personal data, impersonation, unsafe driving and unlawful activity are prohibited. Access may be restricted while serious reports are investigated, subject to fair process and applicable law. Suspension procedures and appeals require business-owner review.

10. Liability and disputes

Concerns about bookings, charges or driver conduct should first be reported to dispatch. Any final allocation of responsibility, dispute-resolution route, governing terms and liability provisions must be reviewed by qualified Nigerian legal counsel. Nothing here is intended to exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

11. Privacy and changes

Our handling of personal information is described in the Privacy Policy. If these terms change, the updated publication date and any legally required notice should be provided.

Use the customer app’s Support option for service enquiries. Ọka Rides is the chosen public-facing business name. Its registered entity/trading-name status, official address and legal contact details remain to be confirmed before publishing these terms as final.
