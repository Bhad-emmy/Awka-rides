FAQ

Quick answers about booking and using Ọka-Rides.

How do I request a ride?

Open Rides or Where to?, enter your pickup point, destination, pickup time and number of passengers, then tap Request Keke.

How is my driver assigned?

Your request first goes to the Ọka-Rides dispatcher. The dispatcher reviews available drivers and manually assigns one to your ride.

How do I know when a driver has been assigned?

Open Activity and select your ride. Once a driver is assigned, you can see the driver's name, vehicle and plate number.

Can I call my driver?

Yes. After a driver has been assigned, open the ride in Activity and tap Call driver.

How do I follow my ride status?

Your ride status is shown in Activity. Open the ride to see its current status and driver details when available.

Can I cancel a ride?

If you need to cancel a ride, contact Ọka-Rides support so the dispatcher can handle the request.

What if I have a payment problem?

Contact Ọka-Rides support with your ride reference and explain the payment issue. The support team can check the ride record.

What if I have a problem with my ride?

Open the ride in Activity and keep your ride reference available, then contact Ọka-Rides support.

How do I contact Ọka-Rides support?

Go to Account → Support. Your phone will open the Ọka-Rides support contact.
